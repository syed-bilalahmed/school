HOW TO USE DURING INSTALLATION:
================================================================================
1. Open your web browser and navigate to:
   http://localhost/school/install/

2. In Step 1 (Welcome & License):
   - Copy any of the 10 keys above and paste it into the "Purchase Code" field.
   - OR select it directly from the "Pre-Authorized Keys" dropdown.
   - Click "Verify Purchase Code".
   - The wizard will display a green checkmark indicating successful verification.

3. Complete the remaining steps (Server Checks, Database Setup, Admin Setup).
4. Upon clicking "Install & Configure System", the verified license will be
   saved in your database (site_settings table) and locked in install.lock.
