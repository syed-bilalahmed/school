SECTION 2

Complete Step-by-Step Operational Pipeline

1
Fee Heads / Types Creation (`fee_types`)

System me pehle fee heads bante hain (e.g. TUI = Tuition Fee, ADM = Admission Fee, LAB = Computer & Science Lab, UTL = Generator & Utility).

fee_types (id, school_id, type_name, type_code, description)
2
Fee Group Packages (`fee_groups` & `fee_groups_types`)

Heads ko group karke package banaya jata hai (e.g. "Class 9th Science Fee Package"). Har head ki specific amount aur fine set ki jati hai.

fee_groups • fee_groups_types (amount, due_date, fine_amount)
3
Class Assignment (`student_fees`)

Jab package class ko assign hota hai, ya koi student enroll hota hai, to student_fees me entry create ho jati hai.

student_fees (student_id, challan_no, billing_month, due_date, status)
4
Payment Recording & Income Sync (`fee_payments` & `incomes`)

Jab cashier fee receive karta hai:

  • fee_payments me transaction record hoti hai (Cash / Bank / Online).
  • student_fees.status update ho kar paid ya partial ho jata hai.
  • Auto Accounting Sync: System automatically school ke Accounting Ledger (incomes) me fee payment ki credit entry daal deta hai!

fee_payments • incomes (Income::syncFeePayment)

Table Name Key Columns Function & Role
fee_types id, school_id, type_name, type_code Fee particulars ki definition (Tuition, Admission, Exam, etc.)
fee_groups id, school_id, group_name, description Classes ke liye fee packages (e.g. Matric Science Package)
fee_groups_types id, fee_group_id, fee_type_id, amount, fine_amount Master link: Kounse group me kounsa head kitni amount ka hai

school_banks bank_name, branch_name, account_title, account_no, iban, is_default Bank challan ke print par aane walay authentic bank accounts
SECTION 4

Regular Monthly Vouchers (Normal Billing)

Open Batch Challans
Baqi Normal Flow Kaise Chalta Hai?

School ke regular enrolled students ke liye monthly cycle bilkul standard chalta hai. Admin /fees/collect ya /fees/challan par jakar "Batch Generate Monthly Vouchers" run karta hai.

Automated Sibling Concession Engine:
  • Pehle bache (1st child) ki regular 100% tuition fee charge hoti hai.
  • Doosre bache (2nd sibling) ko automatically 10% concession milti hai.
  • Teesre aur us se zyada (3rd+ sibling) ko automatically 20% concession milti hai.
  • System duplicate check karta hai taake aik hi mahinay (e.g. October 2026) ka voucher dobara duplicate na bane.
SECTION 5

Fresh / New Admission Fee & Token Hub

Open Admission Form

Jab koi naya student admission leta hai ya online form approve hota hai, to uska Admission Fee Token generate kiya jata hai.

1. Fees Hub Se Token Generator

/fees/collect ke top par `[ 🎟️ Fee Generator & Token ]` click karein.

  • Enrolled Student ya Walk-in Candidate select karein.
  • Admission Registration Fee, Prospectus Token, Security Deposit, 1st Month Tuition heads select karein.
  • Status: Paid at Counter ya Unpaid (Bank Challan).
2. Admission Form Se Token Auto-Link

/students/printAdmission ke top par `[ 🎟️ Fee Token / Challan ]` button dabayein.

  • Candidate ka naam, walid ka naam aur class form se auto pre-fill hotay hain.
  • Token generate hotay hi Section G ke Challan #: ________ me auto fill ho jata hai!
Dual-Copy Authentic Voucher Slip:

Token generate hone par screen par Official Dual-Copy Slip (Office Copy + Student Copy) aati hai jisme School Header, Unique Token # (e.g. TKN-ADM-2026-0042), Candidate Data, Itemized Heads, Paid Stamp aur Authorized Signatures shamil hotay hain jo 1-click se print ho jati hai.

SECTION 6

Fee Register & Live Counter Collection

Open Fee Register

/fees/collect school ka primary counter billing interface hai jo bina page reload kiye (zero freeze) tezi se kaam karta hai:

1. Live Runtime Filter: Class aur Section change karne par table AJAX ke zariye instant update hoti hai.
2. In-Place Payment Modal: Kisi bhi student ke aagay `Collect` click karne par bina page refresh kiye popup khulta hai.
3. Fast Thermal Receipt: Payment save hotay hi official receipt print modal samne aa jata hai.
SECTION 7

3-Copy Bank Challan Hub (`/fees/challan`)

Open Challan Hub

Pakistani standard ke mutabiq har student ka authentic 3-Copy Bank Challan generate hota hai:

1. Bank Copy (بینک کاپی)
2. School Copy (سکول کاپی)
3. Student Copy (طلبہ کاپی)

Challan par verified Bank Account Title, Account No, IBAN (HBL / 1Link), Automated Late Fine, Barcode aur Due Date shamil hoti hai.

SECTION 9

Automatic Accounting & Ledger Integration

System me fees collection alag island nahi hai balkay Accounting Module ke sath mukammal integrated hai:

Auto-Sync Protocol (`Income::syncFeePayment`):

Jaise hi cashier kisi student ki fee receive karta hai (ya admission token mark paid hota hai), backend controller automatically accounting ledger ke table incomes me entry pass kar deta hai. Is se Accounts Officer ko manual double-entry nahi karni parti aur School Income & Expense balance sheet hamesha 100% verified rehti hai.
